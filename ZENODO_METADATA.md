## Public description summary

This preprint presents an independent mathematical investigation motivated by Problem 4.12 in Karlheinz Gröchenig, *Infinite totally positive matrices: some open problems*, Acta Scientiarum Mathematicarum (2026), DOI: 10.1007/s44146-026-00264-3.

For the Gaussian generator \(\phi(x)=e^{-\pi x^2}\), it gives:

1. a lower-density gliding-hump construction with
   \[
   D^-(c)=0,\qquad D^-(Z(f))\ge \tfrac12;
   \]
2. for every \(Q\ge1\), an absolutely summable nowhere-zero coefficient sequence with a fixed periodic sign pattern and
   \[
   D^-(c)=D^+(c)=1/Q,\qquad D^+(Z(f))=\infty;
   \]
3. a Central Gaussian Dominance criterion restoring exact simple-zero/sign-change correspondence and exact preservation of lower and upper densities under explicit amplitude-sensitive sufficient conditions.

Cross-direction density inequalities are not addressed in this release. The work is released for mathematical inspection and independent verification; peer review, formal proof-assistant certification, worldwide novelty, and priority over unidentified prior art are not claimed.
