# Claim Boundary - v2.0

## Claimed in the manuscript

For the Gaussian generator \(\phi(x)=e^{-\pi x^2}\):

1. There exists a bounded real coefficient sequence with
   \[
   D^-(c)=0,\qquad D^-(Z(f))\ge \tfrac12.
   \]
2. For every \(Q\in\mathbb N\), there exists a real sequence \(c\in\ell^1(\mathbb Z)\), nowhere zero, with periodic sign pattern
   \[
   \operatorname{sgn}(c_k)=(-1)^{\lfloor k/Q\rfloor}
   \]
   and
   \[
   D^-(c)=D^+(c)=1/Q,\qquad D^+(Z(f))=\infty.
   \]
3. Under the stated Central Gaussian Dominance hypothesis, each coefficient sign change corresponds to exactly one simple real zero and no other real zeros occur.
4. The lattice-point sufficient conditions in the manuscript imply exact equality of lower and upper zero/sign-change densities.

## Explicitly not claimed

- No theorem for arbitrary Pólya-frequency generators beyond the Gaussian case treated here.
- No claim that Central Gaussian Dominance is necessary or sharp.
- No cross-direction density theorem; in particular, no status claim for \(D^-(Z(f))\le D^+(c)\).
- No sharpening of the lower zero-density bound from \(1/2\) to \(1\) is claimed in v2.0.
- No peer-review status or independent external proof certification.
- No formal proof-assistant verification.
- No worldwide novelty or priority claim over unidentified prior art.
- No use of private correspondence as evidence or authority.

## Release boundary

The public package contains only the canonical manuscript, source, metadata, verification notes, and hashes. Private correspondence, internal chat history, internal handovers, and protected parallel-branch material are excluded.
